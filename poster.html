Problem Statement

Methodology
  • CNN-BiLSTM (Price): 2× Conv1D (64ch, k=3) + BatchNorm + ReLU → 2-layer BiLSTM (h=128, dropout=0.2) on 60-day OHLCV with 21 features including multi-horizon returns, rolling vol, Parkinson/Garman-Klass estimators → z_price ∈ ℝ²⁵⁶.
  • FinBERT (Text): SEC 10-K filings (Items 1, 1A, 7, 7A, 8) chunked into 512-token windows (stride 128), CLS pooling with trained attention weighting → z_text ∈ ℝ⁷⁶⁸. Captures risk disclosures and fundamental context.
  • GAT (Graph): 2-layer Graph Attention Network (4 heads × 64-d, ELU, residual + LayerNorm) on 30-node graph with 7 edge types: supplier, platform/ad/cloud/enterprise competitor, consumer overlap, index correlation → z_graph ∈ ℝ²⁵⁶.
  • Macro MLP: 12 macro features → 3-layer MLP (12→64→64→32) with LayerNorm, GELU, Dropout(0.2) → z_macro ∈ ℝ³². All features lagged 1 day.
  • Sigmoid Gating: Per-sample weighting g ∈ [0,1]⁴ from 2-layer network conditioned on projected modalities + 5-dim earnings surprise vector. Modality mask renormalises when inputs unavailable.
  • HAR-RV Skip: Direct residual path (rv_lag1d/5d/22d → Linear → LayerNorm → GELU → 32-d) bypasses gating — model learns corrections over the classical benchmark. Caused R² jump from 0.867→0.931.
  • Multi-Task Loss: 0.85 × QLIKE (asymmetric: underestimation penalised more) + 0.15 × ListNet (cross-sectional ranking, τ=5.0). Vol head uses Softplus for strict positivity.

Results & Discussion
  1. QLIKE over MSE: Switching loss functions produced the largest single gain (+0.332 R²). QLIKE's asymmetric penalty aligns with financial risk asymmetry — underestimating volatility is costlier than overestimating.
  2. HAR-RV skip connection: The direct residual path from lagged RV to trunk improved R² from 0.867→0.921. The skip bypasses the CNN-BiLSTM compression bottleneck, preserving the strong autoregressive prior.
  3. Deeper vol head: A 5-layer MLP volatility head (256→128→64→32→1) with GELU activations improved R² from 0.921→0.931, closing 98.3% of the benchmark gap.
  4. Multimodal > price-only: Gate weights: price 58.6%, text 19.5%, graph 15.1%, macro 6.8%. All modalities contribute; text and graph are most valuable for directional forecasting.
  5. Direction harder than volatility: AUC=0.593 is consistent with semi-strong EMH — return direction predictability is self-defeating through arbitrage; volatility (2nd moment) lacks this property.
  6. Economic viability: Positive Sharpe through 20bp costs confirms genuine economic signal in the direction predictions.
Limitations & Future Work
  • Universe restricted to 30 US large-cap tech — sector-specific graph limits generalisability.
  • 10-K filings are annual; no intra-year text from 10-Q, 8-K, or earnings calls.
  • Static hand-curated graph; dynamic learned edges could improve expressiveness.
  • Backtest assumes no market impact — real execution faces spreads and liquidity constraints.
  • Test period (2024–25) is a single regime with elevated rates and AI-driven rotation.
